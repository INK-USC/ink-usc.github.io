Readings

Required Readings

Readings will be posted on the course website at the beginning of the course. All materials will be freely and publicly available online.

  • Jurafsky and Martin, Speech and Language Processing, 3rd ed. Chapters on the fundamentals of natural language processing.
  • Eisenstein, Natural Language Processing An overview of machine-learning approaches for NLP.
  • Goldberg, Neural Network Methods for Natural Language Processing. A deep-learning perspective on NLP.
